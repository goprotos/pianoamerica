Our inspection standard

Eighty-eight points.
Signed by name.

Every acoustic piano is examined against the same eighty-eight checks — soundboard, bridges, pinblock, action geometry, hammers, dampers, case and finish. A Registered Piano Technician signs the report with their own name, and you read it before you decide, not after it arrives.

No piano leaves this building with a condition described only in adjectives.
